Insurance & Medi-Cal for ABA Therapy

Many plans, including Medi-Cal, cover ABA therapy for children with an autism diagnosis. Our team will help you understand your coverage and start the authorization process.

How we help with coverage

Insurance can feel like the hardest part of getting started. You don’t have to figure it out alone. When you call us, we can:

  • Walk you through what your plan typically requires for ABA therapy
  • Explain the documents you may need, such as a diagnosis report or referral
  • Help start the authorization process
  • Talk through any expected out-of-pocket costs before services begin

Medi-Cal and ABA therapy

In California, Medi-Cal covers Behavioral Health Treatment (BHT), which includes Applied Behavior Analysis, for members under 21 when services are medically necessary. Only a physician or psychologist can determine that BHT is medically necessary. Members enrolled in a Medi-Cal managed care plan receive BHT through their health plan (California Department of Health Care Services). In Orange County, many Medi-Cal members are enrolled with CalOptima Health.

For a step-by-step explanation, read Does Medi-Cal cover ABA therapy?

Private and employer insurance

Many commercial health plans also cover ABA therapy for autism, but benefits, requirements and costs differ from plan to plan. The quickest way to find out is to call the member services number on your insurance card, or call us and we’ll help you check.

Insurance questions

Does Medi-Cal cover ABA therapy?

Medi-Cal covers Behavioral Health Treatment, including ABA, for members under 21 when it is medically necessary, according to the California Department of Health Care Services. A physician or psychologist determines medical necessity.

Read our Medi-Cal guide →

What do I need before starting ABA with insurance?

Plans usually ask for a diagnosis or recommendation from a physician or psychologist, and prior authorization before therapy starts. Requirements vary, so we help you confirm what your plan needs.

Will I have out-of-pocket costs?

That depends on your plan’s deductible, copays or coinsurance. We will help you understand your benefits before services begin so there are no surprises.

My plan isn’t listed. Can I still call?

Yes. Call (714) 709-7819 and we will check whether we can work with your plan and explain your options.
