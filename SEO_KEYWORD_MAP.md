# SEO Keyword Map — United Autism Therapy

**Market:** Westminster, CA 92683 and Orange County, CA (the only verified service area).
**Research date:** 2026-09-30.

## How this research was done — read first

- **Sources used:** Google autocomplete suggestions (collected for 30+ seed terms), an audit of the current site (unitedautismthereapy.com), and the terminology used by authoritative sources (CDC, NICHD, BACB, ASHA, AOTA, CA DHCS, CA DDS, RCOC).
- **Not available:** no paid keyword tool (Ahrefs, Semrush, Google Keyword Planner) was reachable from the build environment, and web search was disabled.
- **So:** search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC and traffic estimates are **not** included and are marked **Needs verification**. Nothing here is a made-up metric. Before you invest in a keyword, check it in Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account) or Search Console once the site has data.
- **Priority** (High / Medium / Low) is a judgment based on business value (how close the searcher is to contacting a provider), local relevance and how clearly autocomplete showed the phrase exists. It is not based on volume.

Autocomplete signals worth noting:

- `aba therapy westminster` autocompletes alongside **Westminster MD** and **Westminster CO**. Always pair "Westminster" with **CA / California / Orange County** in titles and copy.
- `aba therapy orange county` also suggests "orange county **ny**", so include "CA" there too.
- Parents search both `aba therapy near me` and `aba therapy near me that accepts medicaid`, and `does medi-cal cover aba therapy` is a real query. **Coverage content is high-value.**
- `what is aba therapy and why is it controversial` and `aba therapy controversy` are prominent. We answer them honestly (on /blog/is-aba-therapy-controversial/) instead of ignoring them.
- `aba therapy in vietnamese` appears. Westminster is home to Little Saigon, so Vietnamese-language content could be a real local opportunity. **Only pursue it if the business confirms Vietnamese-speaking staff.**

### Cannibalization decisions made

- **"Questions to ask before starting ABA"** (a suggested topic) was merged into **How to choose an ABA provider**. Both targeted the same intent ("questions to ask ABA providers").
- **"How speech therapy can support communication"** was merged into **Speech therapy for autism** (same intent).
- **"Autism support for parents: where to start"** was made local (Orange County), so it doesn't compete with the ASD explainer.
- **"ABA therapy for toddlers"** was folded into **What age to start ABA therapy** (as a secondary keyword) instead of getting its own page.
- **Added, based on autocomplete:** "Does Medi-Cal cover ABA therapy?" and "Why is ABA therapy controversial?"
- **Service pages vs. blog posts:** service pages own the local/commercial phrasing ("… in Westminster, CA"). Blog posts own the question phrasing ("what is…", "how does…").
