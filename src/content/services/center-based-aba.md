## Why families choose center-based ABA

Center-based ABA takes place at our center at 13571 Beach Blvd in Westminster. The setting is designed for learning: predictable routines, a range of materials and activities, and a team that is on site together. For many children, it offers a good balance of focused one-on-one teaching and natural chances to be around other kids.

Center-based therapy can be a good fit when your child:

- Is getting ready for preschool, transitional kindergarten or a new classroom
- Would benefit from practicing skills around other children
- Learns best with fewer distractions than home sometimes has
- Is working on following group routines, waiting and transitions

It is not the right fit for everyone, and it does not have to be all-or-nothing. Some families combine center sessions with [in-home ABA](/services/in-home-aba/) so skills learned at the center are practiced at home. Read [center-based vs. in-home ABA](/blog/center-based-vs-in-home-aba-therapy/) for a side-by-side look.

## What a day at the center looks like

Each session is planned from your child’s treatment plan. A typical visit might include:

- **Warm-up and connection.** Your child’s therapist starts with activities your child enjoys so the session begins on a positive note.
- **Teaching built into play.** Skills like requesting, labeling, imitating or turn-taking are practiced during games, puzzles, songs and pretend play.
- **Structured practice.** Short, focused teaching moments for skills that need extra repetition.
- **Social opportunities.** When it fits your child’s goals, guided activities with peers, like sharing materials or a simple group game.
- **Breaks and regulation.** Movement, calm-down time and choice are part of the day.
- **Data and review.** Therapists record progress during the session so the BCBA can see what is working.

For a closer look at a session from start to finish, see [what happens in an ABA therapy session](/blog/what-happens-in-an-aba-therapy-session/).

## Supervision and communication

Your child’s program is designed and supervised by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA). Sessions are delivered by Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs) under that supervision. You will have regular check-ins to talk about progress, ask questions and decide on next goals together. [Parent training](/services/parent-training/) is included so you can use the same strategies at home.
