## Support in the classroom

School brings its own set of demands: following group directions, moving between activities, waiting, working near other children and handling a busy, noisy environment. School-based ABA helps your child practice those skills in the classroom, where they are actually needed.

We provide school-based support for children in Westminster and across Orange County, where the school permits it. A therapist can support your child during parts of the school day, and your child’s BCBA can share strategies so expectations stay consistent between home, school and therapy.

## Skills we often focus on at school

- Following classroom routines and group instructions
- Transitioning between activities or locations
- Asking for help, a break or a needed item
- Participating in circle time, centers or small groups
- Playing and communicating with classmates during recess
- Using coping strategies when the environment feels overwhelming

Every goal is set by your child’s BCBA with your input, based on an assessment of what your child needs.

## Working with your child’s school

School-based services require the school’s permission, and policies vary between schools and districts. With your consent, we can coordinate with teachers and staff so everyone uses similar strategies.

It helps to understand how private ABA fits alongside your child’s school supports. An **IEP (Individualized Education Program)** is created by your school district under the [Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA)](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/). School-based ABA from a private provider is a separate service. It does not replace your child’s IEP, but it can support goals your child is working on at school. If you are just beginning with school supports, our guide to [autism support for parents in Orange County](/blog/autism-support-orange-county-parents/) explains where to start.

## Part of a connected plan

School-based ABA works best as part of a bigger picture. Many children also receive [center-based](/services/center-based-aba/) or [in-home ABA](/services/in-home-aba/), and caregivers take part in [parent training](/services/parent-training/). Keeping strategies consistent across settings gives your child more chances to practice.

Want to know whether school-based support is possible for your child? [Request a consultation](/contact/) and we will talk through your child’s situation and school.
