Center-Based ABA

Center-based ABA takes place at our location at 13571 Beach Blvd in Westminster. The setting is designed for learning, with predictable routines, a range of materials and a team on site together.

  • One-on-one teaching built into play, with BCBA oversight
  • Chances to practice social skills around other children
  • Helpful preparation for preschool and classroom routines
  • Can be combined with in-home sessions
Learn about center-based ABA Call (714) 709-7819

In-Home ABA

In-home ABA brings therapy to your home. Your child works on goals during the routines that matter most — meals, play, getting dressed and bedtime — while you learn strategies alongside the therapist.

  • Skills taught where your child actually uses them
  • Goals built around your family’s daily routines
  • Plans designed and supervised by BCBAs, delivered with RBTs
  • Available across Orange, Riverside, San Diego, San Bernardino and Santa Clara Counties
Learn about in-home ABA Call (714) 709-7819

Parent Training

Parent training is hands-on coaching led by your child’s BCBA. You learn practical strategies, see them modeled, then practice with feedback — so your child gets consistent support every day.

  • Strategies for routines, communication and challenging moments
  • Explain, show, practice and feedback — not just handouts
  • Grandparents and other caregivers can often join
  • Included in our ABA programs
Learn about parent training Call (714) 709-7819

School-Based ABA

School-based ABA helps your child practice classroom skills — following routines, transitions, asking for help and joining group activities — in the place they are needed, where the school permits it.

  • Support for routines, transitions and group participation
  • Collaboration with teachers and staff, with your consent
  • Consistent strategies across home, center and school
  • A separate service from your child’s IEP that can support its goals
Learn about school-based ABA Call (714) 709-7819

ABA in everyday life

Learning that fits into your family’s day

At home, ordinary moments become chances to practice. Your child’s BCBA may build goals around:

  • Mealtime — requesting food, sitting with the family, trying new foods
  • Play — taking turns, sharing toys, joining a game
  • Getting dressed — following the steps, choosing clothes, asking for help
  • Hygiene — handwashing, brushing teeth, bath-time routines
  • Transitions — leaving the park, ending screen time, getting in the car

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about in-home ABA

Do your therapists come to our home?

Yes. In-home ABA is our core service. Your child’s therapist comes to your home for scheduled sessions, and your BCBA supervises the program, reviews progress and coaches caregivers. We serve families across Orange, Riverside, San Diego, San Bernardino and Santa Clara Counties.

In-home ABA therapy →

What is ABA therapy?

ABA (Applied Behavior Analysis) is an evidence-based approach that uses positive reinforcement to help children build communication, social, play and daily living skills, and to reduce behaviors that get in the way of learning. Every plan is individualized, and progress is measured along the way.

Read our parent’s guide to ABA →

Who will work with my child?

Treatment plans are designed and supervised by Board Certified Behavior Analysts (BCBAs). Day-to-day sessions are delivered by Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs) working under that supervision.

BCBA vs RBT: who does what →

Is ABA therapy covered by insurance or Medi-Cal?

Many plans, including Medi-Cal, cover ABA therapy for children with an autism diagnosis. Coverage and requirements vary by plan. Call (714) 709-7819 and our team will help you understand your coverage and start the authorization process.

Does a parent need to be home during sessions?

Yes, a parent or caregiver generally needs to be home during in-home sessions. Being nearby also lets you see strategies in action and practice them with support from the team.
