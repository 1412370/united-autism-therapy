Our Autism Therapy Services

Three core therapies that work together to support communication, social development, independence and behavioral growth.

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA)

Individualized, data-driven therapy using positive reinforcement to build skills and reduce challenging behaviors.

  • Individualized behavior plans
  • Positive reinforcement strategies
  • Communication & social skill building
  • Regular BCBA progress reviews
Learn more →

Speech & Language Therapy

Helping children develop verbal and non-verbal communication so they can express themselves fully.

  • Verbal & non-verbal communication
  • Articulation & language comprehension
  • Social & pragmatic language skills
  • AAC support when needed
Learn more →

Occupational Therapy (OT)

Building independence through fine motor development, sensory integration and self-care skill training.

  • Sensory integration therapy
  • Fine motor & coordination skills
  • Daily living & self-care skills
  • School readiness support
